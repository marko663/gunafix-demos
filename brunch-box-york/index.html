Story

Made
while you wait

We are a small traditional takeaway sandwich shop serving the local and business community in and around Micklegate, York. Whether you are a tradesman after an early hot sandwich or you work in an office or shop, we will be happy to serve you.

As far as possible we source locally and support Yorkshire businesses, with preserves, pickles and dressings from Brackenhill Fine Foods, Pipers Crisps and Tarte and Berry triple chocolate brownies.
