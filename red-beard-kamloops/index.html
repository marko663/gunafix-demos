Story

A place
for visiting

Red Beard is part pub, part cafe, part restaurant: a place for visiting. We pour from 18 rotating taps the best craft beers and ciders we can find, cook brunch, lunch and dinner from scratch, and serve crafted cocktails and third wave coffee.

Come by for a date, with friends, with the kids or with the dog on the patio, and visit.
