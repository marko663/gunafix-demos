Story

Where locals
feel at home

Located in beautiful Port Colborne, Bambi's is the bistro where the locals go to feel at home, dine on fresh food and socialize.

Homemade, flavourful soups, fresh sandwiches and a variety of baked goods. We cater to all types of dietary requirements, take orders and make treat boxes to go.
