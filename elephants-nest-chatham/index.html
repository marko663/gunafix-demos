Story

A downtown
staple

Founded in 1996, The Elephant's Nest is a staple in downtown Chatham, with over 10 beers on tap, a large selection of craft beers, coolers and wines, and a large menu.

We can host large groups for your celebration, team outing, quick lunch break or after-work drinks and dinner. Hedley, The Trews and The Tea Party have all played here. Catch the game on one of our 8 TVs.
