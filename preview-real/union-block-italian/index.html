On theLanding

Located on the Landing of Birkett Mills and below the Water Street Wine Bar, Union Block Italian serves up homestyle Italian cuisine with a Mediterranean influence.

Classics like lasagna and shrimp scampi live alongside original creations and housemade dressings and sauces. Enjoy local craft beers, wines and specialty cocktails in the rustic bar and dining room, or out on the patio.
