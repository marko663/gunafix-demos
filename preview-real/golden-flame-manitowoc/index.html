Since May 23, 1984

The Golden Flame Family Restaurant is one of the longest operating casual dining spots in Manitowoc, Wisconsin. Salvador and Sue Velasques opened the doors on May 23, 1984, and it is still family-owned and operated.

Many of our waitstaff and employees have been here since our doors opened. Our stoves and ovens burn all day long, so no matter what time you join us, it's fresh.
