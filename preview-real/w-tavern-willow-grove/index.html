The Willow Inn,reimagined

Introducing the new and improved W Tavern 19090, previously known as the Willow Inn. Continuing the family-run business that has been around since 1949, the space was reinvented using the original structure, with a modern twist.

Everything on the menu is homemade. The original chef from the Willow Inn, there for more than 20 years, kept some of his traditional items, and Chef Andrei added new creations: elevated bar food with an Italian touch.
