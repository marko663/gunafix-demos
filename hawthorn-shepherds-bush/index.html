FormerlyThe British Queen

The Hawthorn, formerly known as The Pocket Watch or The British Queen, was reopened by Peter Creed and Tom Noest, who run five other pubs in the Cotswolds.

The focus is simple pub food with a straightforward wine list and a good spirit and beer selection. Produce comes, as much as possible, from the same Cotswold suppliers as the other pubs.

Sister pubs
The Bell, Langford · The Lamb, Shipton · The Fox, Broadwell · The Sherborne Arms, Northleach · The Tivoli, Cheltenham
