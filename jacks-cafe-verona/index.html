Story

Our
story

Jack's Cafe was founded by Jack and Marianne in 1997. Jack had always had a love for cooking and his dream was to one day own his own restaurant. With the help of his wife, Marianne, the two made Jack's dream a reality.

When you walk through the doors, we want you to feel like you are a part of the Jack's family. We would not be where we are today without the love and support of our family, friends and community.
