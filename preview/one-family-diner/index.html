Eggs and home fries to cachupa and banana bread French toast; family-owned on Bowdoin Street since 1992.

One Family Diner, on Bowdoin Street in Dorchester, has all the great breakfast delights you could ever want. A family-owned restaurant serving top-quality food since 1992.

We take great pride in sharing our passion for food, drinks and time with family, friends and colleagues. Tables are first come, first served; order ahead by calling 617-436-1577.

The kitchen opens at 5:30am on weekdays and 6am on weekends. Specials come with home fries or grits, toast and coffee.
