Slow roasted, after dark

Nachtbohne is a coffee house that opens when the city slows down. We roast in small batches after closing and pour until midnight: espresso, slow bar, and things to eat with them.

01 — The night shift

Every cup starts after closing time.

01

Roast

When the last guest leaves, the little drum roaster wakes up. Six kilos at a time, by ear and by nose, until the first crack sounds like rain on a window.

6 kgper batch
12 minaverage roast
3origins on rotation
02

Rest

Fresh beans need a few days to calm down. Ours rest in the window, in jars with the roast date written on by hand.

03

Pour

Espresso pulled slow, filter poured by hand, oat milk steamed until it shines. Then the cup goes on the saucer and the night begins.
