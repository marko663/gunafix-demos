## First-party pages used
- https://cakeviewbakery.webflow.io/ (home)
- /about, /cakes, /egg-tarts, /menu, /contact on the same site

## Facts taken from them
- Since 1996 story, 'Home of the Fresh Mango Cake', cake and egg-tart names (incl. Chinese names) and $ prices, 6-inch / +$2 plaque / prices subject to change notes, custom cakes and catering, delivery note, hours daily 8am–8pm, address, phone, Gmail.
- Menu note shown on the page: Prices from Cakeview's own site. Cake prices are for 6" cakes (from), +$2 for a chocolate plaque message, and are subject to change.
- Hours note shown on the page: Monday to Sunday, 8am to 8pm. Hours may vary on public holidays.

## Website issue (for the outreach email)
- The site is still on a webflow.io staging subdomain with template placeholders ('Product name', croissant/scone copy) and 'Buns (coming soon!)'.

## Not shown / not invented
- No reviews, ratings, awards, delivery promises or prices that are not on the venue's own pages.
- No customer photos and no Google Maps photos (Maps photos could not be opened from the box; the palette comes from the venue's own site, signage and setting).
- The venue's own photos and logo are not republished.
