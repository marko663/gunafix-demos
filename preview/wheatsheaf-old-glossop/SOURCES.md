## First-party pages used
- https://glossopwheatsheaf.wixsite.com/mysite (home)
- /about-us, /menu, /what-s-on on the same site
- https://www.facebook.com/Wheatsheah.OG (linked from the site's menu page as the weekly menu)

## Facts taken from them
- Food, drink, atmosphere and location texts, renovation since August 2018, opening hours and food-serving hours, address, phone, Gmail, dog friendly, beer garden.
- Menu note shown on the page: The Wheatsheaf has no set menu: dishes change weekly and this week's menu is posted on the pub's Facebook page. The kitchen is closed on Monday and Tuesday.
- Hours note shown on the page: Food serving hours: Wednesday to Saturday 12:30–8pm, Sunday 12:30–7pm. Kitchen closed Monday and Tuesday.

## Website issue (for the outreach email)
- Free wixsite.com address titled 'Mysite' (© 2023) with the Wix ad banner; the menu page only points to Facebook.

## Not shown / not invented
