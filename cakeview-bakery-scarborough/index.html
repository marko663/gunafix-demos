Since 1996

A cozy community hub

Established with a heartfelt vision and a sprinkle of passion, Cakeview has been delighting the community with homemade treats since 1996, drawing on beloved family customs.

Cakes, buns and pastries are made with fresh, premium ingredients and traditional techniques, and baked fresh daily. More than a bakery: a cozy community hub in the heart of Scarborough.
