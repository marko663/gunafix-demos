Skoosh is a cafe on Main Street in Drymen serving quality food and drinks, with coffee that people love, made from 100% Fair Trade beans.

Come in for a full Scottish breakfast, a fresh baguette at lunchtime, a scone in the afternoon or an ice cream, open seven days a week.
