Next tothe station

Cafe 159 Kitchen & Bistro sits at 159 Victoria Street, right next to St Albans train station: a go-to spot for fresh breakfasts, great coffee and friendly service.

The menu runs all day, from the 159 Special and Eggs Royale to gourmet burgers, paninis, salads, milkshakes and smoothies.
