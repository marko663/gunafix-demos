Kosher,in the park

Welcome to Hendon Park Cafe, the first and only kosher park cafe in the UK. There is plenty of outdoor seating in the sunshine and indoor seating for all weather, and the cafe is open all year round.

The menu runs from the famous full English breakfast, shakshuka and Israeli platters to cappuccinos, smoothies and coke floats, with a children's menu to excite the youngest members of the community.
