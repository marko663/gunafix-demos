D’eCaffé

Italian bakery & café

Croissants, sfogliatelle & espresso on East Park Ave

D’eCaffé is an Italian bakery and café at 151 East Park Avenue in Long Beach. Mornings start early from Tuesday, with the pastry case full of croissants, from pistachio and almond to chocolate and Nutella.

Guests come back for croissant sandwiches, pistachio éclairs, sfogliatelle, lobster tails, tiramisu and rainbow cookies, plus a cup of coffee to go with them. Custom cakes are made to order.
