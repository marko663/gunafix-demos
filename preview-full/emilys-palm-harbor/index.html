Our story

At Emily’s you’re always family

For over 35 years Emily’s has served breakfast and lunch to neighborhood locals and tourists alike in Pinellas County. Today it’s owned and managed by the Zenku family.

Mark Zenku has spent decades in restaurants, from a special-events venue in Chicago’s Lincoln Park to an upscale steakhouse in Clearwater. You’ll likely meet him at the front desk or chatting with guests over their meals.

“Whether you’re visiting for the first time or a long time customer, you’ll always be treated like family.”
