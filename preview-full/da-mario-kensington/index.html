Our food is made with love

A Kensington Italian, the classic way.

Da Mario’s menu reads like a trattoria should: bruschetta and garlic pizza bread to start, pappardelle with beef ragù cooked for 24 hours, aubergine parmigiana baked in the pizza oven, and a long list of pizzas built on San Marzano tomatoes.

Every dish on this page comes from Da Mario’s own published menu.
