Klines Court

A small breakfast and lunch room, just off the corner

Meta Café is at 15 Klines Court, Lambertville, New Jersey. Its own Toast page describes it as a little café tucked away in the cozy corner of Klines Court. The Peasant Wife, writing in January 2026, names Ella Dsouza and Aldrin Phillips, and earlier in October 2025 noted there is no official website.

Guests can call, come in, or order on the café’s Toast page, which is linked throughout this preview. This page is a GunaFix preview, not the official site.
